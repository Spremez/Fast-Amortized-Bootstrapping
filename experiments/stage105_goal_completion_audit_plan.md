# Stage105 Goal Completion Audit Plan

Date: 2026-06-30

## Goal

Audit the active PVW/MAT-SAB objective requirement by requirement and decide
whether the scoped systems/engineering SAB acceleration evidence chain is
complete.

## Inputs

- Stage20 active-buffer/copyback fusion evidence.
- Stage36 target performance, noise, and resource evidence.
- Stage97 scalar/default source isolation guard.
- Stage101 native perf counter evidence.
- Stage102 2025/686 source-anchor review.
- Stage103 related-work/novelty boundary review.
- Stage104 post-external final package.
- Stage42 closure verifier, run log, artifact manifest, and reproduction
  checklist.

## Gates

- Every scoped requirement must map to direct evidence.
- Stronger unsupported claims must remain explicitly blocked.
- The audit must not convert scoped evidence into all-parameter, non-binary,
  theoretical-optimality, or broad novelty wording.

## Claim Policy

Passing Stage105 means the scoped systems/engineering evidence chain is
complete. It does not mean broader paper-level novelty or theoretical
optimality claims are complete.
